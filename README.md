# Third Party Risk Management (TPRM) Portfolio
Third Party Risk Management(TPRM) portfolio demonstrating vendor assessment, risk evaluation etc.

## Project Scenario

A fictional organization is onboarding a third party SaaS vendor that will process sensitive business and customer information. This project demonstrates how a GRC professional can assess the vendor security posture, identify and evaluate risk, document findings, recommend remediation and support ongoing third-party risk monitoring.

## TPRM Lifecycle Demonstrated

Vendor Identification and Criticality,
Due Diligence and Security Assessment,
Risk Matrix Calculation,
Risk Identification and Evaluation,
Risk Scoring,
Control Assessment,
Risk Acceptance and Approvals,
Vendor Onboarding and Contract Signing,
Continuous Monitoring,
Remediation Efforts,
Incident Response Plan,
Vendor Offboarding,

## Portfolio Deliverables

Vendor Criticality Assessment,
Third Party Security Questionnaires,
Vendor Risk Assessment Methodology,
Vendor Risk Register,
Risk Scoring Methodology,
Remediation and Treatment Plan,
Vendor Risk Report and Metrics,
Vendor Offboarding Process,
Ongoing Monitoring Program Plan (vendor re-evaluation),
